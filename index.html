01 / Case study

Madinah Operations Intelligence

An independent operations intelligence case study built with 30,000 synthetic municipal service reports: SLA reporting, team capacity, and prediction-risk review.

AI / PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

Model review: I removed four capacity inputs whose availability at report creation was unverified and corrected 645 labels unavailable at their original cutoff. On 6,057 resolved test reports, recall improved to 85.3%, but precision fell to 33.6%: 148 more true positives and 611 more false alerts.
